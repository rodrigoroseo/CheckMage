The original asset pack contains 12 pixel-art fantasy character sprites.
The sprites are used as chess-piece artwork in this project. The original PNG
is retained unchanged; a copy with the outside background made transparent
and a tight-crop manifest are generated by scripts/prepare-heroes-sprites.mjs.
